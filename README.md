# Product Portfolio — Soumi Ghosh

A product portfolio built to demonstrate product thinking, not just list projects.

---

## The Problem

Most PM portfolios are project galleries. They show what was built, but not *how decisions were made* or *why certain paths were chosen over others*.

Recruiters and hiring managers need to evaluate:
- How does this person think about product problems?
- Can they prioritize under constraints?
- Do they understand trade-offs?
- Can they communicate decisions clearly?

A resume shows credentials. A portfolio should show *thinking*.

## Design Philosophy

**Editorial, not flashy**  
The design prioritizes readability and clarity over visual effects. This is a portfolio for product managers, not a design showcase.

**Content-first structure**  
Every section is designed to answer a specific question a recruiter might have:
- *Can they find real problems?* → Case studies start with problem framing
- *Do they understand users?* → Evidence sections show research and data
- *Can they prioritize?* → RICE frameworks and explicit trade-offs
- *Can they ship?* → MVP definitions and rollout plans

**Progressive disclosure**  
Summaries on portfolio → Full case studies in Notion → Source docs on request

## Product Decisions Made

### ✅ What I Chose to Build

**Case study structure over project galleries**  
Most portfolios list projects with brief descriptions. I chose to show *how* decisions were made, not just *what* was built.

**Notion integration**  
Full case studies live in Notion (native format for PMs). The portfolio shows summaries and links out for depth.

**Technical background as differentiator**  
Instead of hiding engineering experience, I positioned it as a PM advantage: better constraint understanding, faster prototyping, clearer communication with eng teams.

### ❌ What I Explicitly Didn't Build

**Visual portfolio pieces**  
No Dribbble-style mockups. This isn't a designer portfolio.

**Generic "About Me" storytelling**  
Replaced soft intro with concrete: "Product Manager with a builder's background. Before product, I built software."

**Metrics without context**  
Every number includes *why it matters* and *how it was measured*.
